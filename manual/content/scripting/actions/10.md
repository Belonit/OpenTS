summary: Hands the whole screen over to a movie and returns the mission when it finishes.
caveats:
  - Only a campaign game shows the movie. A skirmish or any multiplayer session reaches the action, passes over the movie without a word, and carries on with the rest of the trigger's actions.
  - A movie that cannot be found, cannot be opened, or is too small to be shown is passed over just as silently.
  - The escape key cuts the movie short and the mission resumes from that point. No other key does.
  - The screen is filled black before the movie and again after it, and the whole display is redrawn once the mission comes back.

## What happens to the music

The action names no music track of its own, so nothing is queued behind the movie, and nothing is faded or stopped on the action's account. Movie audio uses a dedicated playback buffer and does not rebuild the game's shared sound output to match the soundtrack format.

Full-screen playback holds the main game loop, so no more music data is read while the movie is open. The data buffered before the movie continues playing and may run out before the movie ends. A compressed track normally has between six and eleven seconds buffered; an uncompressed track holds about a quarter as much time.

Music handling resumes with the mission. If the buffered data ran out, the interrupted track does not continue: the game detects that nothing is playing and starts the next track from its beginning. [Movie playback](/systems/movie-playback/) covers the movie's own audio and timing.
