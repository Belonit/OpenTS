---
command_id: Diplomacy
---

Opens the [diplomacy panel](/systems/diplomacy/), or closes it when it is open. The panel opens in a skirmish or a game against other machines, and the command does nothing in a campaign.

A [`KEYBOARD.INI`](/formats/keyboard-ini/) entry named `ToggleRadar`, from before the panel replaced the radar pane's player list, binds this command.
