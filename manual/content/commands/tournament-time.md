---
command_id: launch:tournament-time
---

Give every player's copy the same value: each copy checks only its own `-TIME=`, and decides only for its own player. When the time runs out, the player with the highest score wins and every other player loses. Observers do not count, and of two tied players only one wins.

The limit counts game minutes of 900 frames from the start of the match. Campaign and skirmish games ignore it.
