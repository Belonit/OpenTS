The EVA line that plays when an object of a house the player controls is promoted to veteran or elite rank in an announced promotion. [Promotion feedback](/systems/veterancy/#promotion-feedback) lists which promotions are announced and which are done silently.

A unit being promoted from below rookie to rookie is never announced.

An unrecognized EVA line doesn't play anything.
