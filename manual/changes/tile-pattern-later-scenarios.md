---
title: Pick each scenario's terrain tile arrangement afresh
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [ZivDero]
---

The random arrangement of terrain tile variations, such as those of clear ground, used to be picked once per session. Every scenario after the first reused the first scenario's arrangement and made its other cosmetic random choices differently than in a fresh session. Each scenario now picks its own arrangement and looks the way it would as the first scenario of a session.
